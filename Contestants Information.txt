# BIGG BOSS 20 — REAL-TIME CONTESTANT INTELLIGENCE AGENT

## Objective

Build and maintain a verified, continuously updated intelligence database for Bigg Boss Hindi Season 20 (2026).

**Research all 18 currently identified contestants**, including active, eliminated, wildcard and re-entered participants. Do not assume that a fan website's roster is authoritative.

## Core requirements

### 1. Verify the contestant roster
- Establish the complete contestant list from the premiere onward.
- Determine who is currently inside the house.
- Record evictions, wildcards, re-entries and changes to contestant status.
- Cross-check official JioHotstar and Colors TV content against established entertainment journalism.
- Attach sources and last-verified timestamps to every status.

### 2. Build individual contestant dossiers

For each contestant, collect:

- Full legal or publicly documented name and stage name
- Profession, career history and notable achievements
- Publicly documented age, hometown and background
- Television, film, music, sports or digital career
- Previous reality-show appearances and outcomes
- Verified social media handles and official photographs
- Significant pre-show controversies, with evidence
- Public statements and responses to controversies

### 3. Track Bigg Boss performance

Maintain a chronological episode-by-episode record of:

- Task participation, wins and failures
- Captaincy nominations and victories
- Weekly nominations, immunity and eliminations
- Rule violations and official punishments
- Arguments, physical confrontations and resolutions
- Alliances, friendships, rivalries and betrayals
- Weekend Ka Vaar feedback
- Positive and negative contributions to the game

Do not substitute social-media popularity for actual task performance.

### 4. Track public sentiment

Collect publicly accessible reactions from:

- Instagram comments and official posts
- YouTube episode discussions and comments
- X posts and discussion threads
- Reddit discussions
- Facebook public posts
- Established entertainment news publications

Classify sentiment as positive, negative, neutral or mixed. Capture the volume, source, timestamp and representative themes.

Identify bots, coordinated campaigns, duplicated comments and promotional content where possible.

Never represent an unscientific fan poll as an official voting result.

### 5. Evidence hierarchy

**Primary:** Official episode footage, live feeds, Colors TV and JioHotstar announcements, verified contestant statements.

**Secondary:** India Today, The Indian Express, Hindustan Times, Pinkvilla, Times of India and other established outlets.

**Supplementary:** BiggBoss Pulse, fan communities, Reddit, social-media discussions and entertainment blogs.

BiggBoss Pulse may be used as a reference for features and discovery, but not as the sole source of truth.

### 6. Data quality rules

- No fabricated statistics, votes, rankings or controversies.
- Distinguish allegations from verified incidents.
- Preserve conflicting reports and their sources.
- Record publication date separately from event date.
- Never treat engagement as an official vote count.
- Mark unverifiable fields as `unknown`.
- Keep source URLs and evidence timestamps.
- Do not use AI-generated contestant images as documentary evidence.

### 7. Refresh frequency

- Official roster and eviction status: after each episode and official announcement.
- Episode performance: after each broadcast.
- Social sentiment: every 3–6 hours where platform access permits.
- Career and biography: when reliable new information appears.
- Controversies: when new evidence, responses or resolutions emerge.

### 8. Required outputs

1. Current contestant roster
2. Individual career and biography dossiers
3. Verified controversy timeline
4. Episode-by-episode performance history
5. Contestant relationships and rivalry network
6. Weekly public sentiment trends
7. Source reliability and conflict report
8. Structured JSON/database records with update timestamps

## Acceptance criteria

Every displayed fact must be traceable to evidence. The system must be able to explain why it considers a contestant active, eliminated, controversial, popular or successful in a task.

**Priority: accuracy, evidence and freshness over speed or sensationalism.**
